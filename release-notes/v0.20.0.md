## Chat windows

- **The plugin's own chat windows,** to use alongside or instead of the game's. They're off to start. `/dap chat` shows and hides the main window.
- **Tabs that pop out.** The main window has Tells, Group, Channels and Combat tabs, and each can pop out into a window of its own. Each window picks which of the game's chat categories it shows and who you talk to when you type in it.
- **Sharp text at any size,** in any font you have installed, with timestamps and colours for each chat type and channel.
- **Don't miss anything.** Your name and words you choose are highlighted, and tabs show an unread count and flash.
- **Copy, find and save.** Hover over a line to copy it, search what's there, or save chat to files.
- **Hide game chat** (Client UI Override) hides the game's chat windows, and Enter types in the plugin's box instead.
- **Links work here too.** Links from the game and other plugins work as usual, and clicking a name starts a tell.
- **Combat you've hidden from chat** still shows in a chat window with Combat or Magic ticked, so you can keep it out of the game's chat and read it here.

## Item links

- **Link in chat.** Right-click an item and choose **Link in chat** to share it. Only `[Name]` goes in the line, so players without the plugin just see the name.
- **Hover over a link** to see the item, or click it to open it in the Appraisal window, compared with what you're wearing.
- **Link it again** passes on a link someone else shared.

## Themes

- **Choose a look for the plugin's windows** in the Config tab's new **Theme** section: Daralet, UtilityBelt, Dereth Parchment, Midnight, Ocean, Forest, Crimson, Amethyst, Slate or High Contrast. The choice is saved with your settings profile.
- **Daralet is the new default,** so the plugin will look different after updating. Its near-black browns, gold borders, dark blue buttons and red selections come from the game's own panels. Pick **UtilityBelt** to go back to the old look.
- **Other plugins keep their own look.**

## Windows

- **Resize the attached shared window.** With **Attach to toolbar** on, drag the window's free edge to set its height. Double-click the edge to go back to the height of the game's panels.
- **See your version.** The window's top line now shows the version you have, after your character's name. Hover over it to see the result of the update check.

## Looting and selling

- **Loot all no longer gets stuck on quest drops.** When the server refuses an item because of its quest, the looter leaves it and any others of that name in the corpse, says so in chat, and carries on with the rest.
- **Drag your main pack onto a vendor** to put its items on the sell list. Worn items and side packs are left out.

## Good to know

- **Item links need the matching server update.**
- **Item links in General and Fellowship chat may not work.** They were seen not to open there while Trade worked, and the cause hasn't been found yet.
- **Keep the typing box if you hide the game's chat.** With **Typing box** unticked and **Hide game chat** on, Enter leaves you nowhere to type.
